      How to play
      Phone
      Drag the left stick to walk. Tap Fireball to shoot where your warlock faces (follow the arrow). Tap any other spell to fire it at the nearest rival, or drag it to aim and let go to cast. Drag back to the button to cancel.
      Move
      Right-click (or tap) the ground. Hold right-click to steer.
      Cast
      Press Q W E R T A S D F. Spells fly toward your cursor. On touch, tap a spell, then tap a target.
      Lava
      Burns 14 HP a second. The lower your HP, the farther every hit throws you.
      Ring
      Crumbles every 12 seconds. Fireballs cancel each other out in mid-air.
      Gold
      Earned by surviving, killing and dealing damage. Spend it between rounds.
