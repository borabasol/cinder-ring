      How to play
      Move
      Right-click (or tap) the ground. Hold right-click to steer.
      Cast
      Press Q W E R A S D F. Spells fly toward your cursor. On touch, tap a spell, then tap a target.
      Lava
      Burns 14 HP a second. The lower your HP, the farther every hit throws you.
      Ring
      Crumbles every 12 seconds. Fireballs cancel each other out in mid-air.
